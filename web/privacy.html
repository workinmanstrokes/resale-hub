Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information the Resale Hub mobile app and its online service (together, “Resale Hub”) collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. Resale Hub is developed by Diddlerrr (“we”, “us”). Contact: mscofield1588@gmail.com.

Who Resale Hub is for

Resale Hub is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Selling on eBay is subject to eBay’s own age requirements.

How we use information

Only to run Resale Hub’s features: signing in, saving listings, sending photos to eBay, publishing to eBay when you ask, searching marketplaces, keeping the service secure, and answering support requests. We do not sell or rent personal information, show ads, use advertising identifiers, track you across other apps or websites, or use third-party analytics SDKs.

Who we share it with

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Hosting) processes data on our behalf to run the service, in the United States. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Vercel runs our API server, which relays requests between the app, Firebase and eBay. It does not store your listings or photos. See Vercel’s privacy policy.
  • eBay, when you connect your account and publish. We send the listing details, photos, price and your selected policies to eBay. Data you send to eBay is governed by eBay’s privacy notice.
  • Etsy receives the search terms you enter (and our app key) to return public listings. See Etsy’s privacy policy.
  • Authorities, if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

Retention and deletion

  • Listings are kept until you delete them. Deleting a listing removes it and its preview; you can also choose to end the live eBay listing at the same time. Photos hosted by eBay stay with a live eBay listing; eBay deletes photos that aren’t used in a listing on its own after its expiry period.
  • Disconnecting eBay (Account → eBay → Disconnect) deletes your stored eBay tokens and settings.
  • Deleting your account (Account → Delete account, or see this page) permanently deletes your account, listings, photo previews, eBay tokens and settings right away. Items already live on eBay stay on eBay and can be managed in eBay Seller Hub. Residual server logs expire within 30 days.

Your rights and choices

You can view and edit your listings in the app, disconnect eBay, and delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California privacy laws) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict processing. Email mscofield1588@gmail.com from your account email and we will respond within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Data is protected by Firebase security rules so each user can access only their own data, eBay tokens are never sent to the app, and the API applies per-user rate limits. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
