Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to search. To create listings and publish to eBay, sign up with your email in the Account tab.

How do I connect eBay?

Go to Account → eBay → Connect eBay and sign in on eBay’s own page. Resale Hub never sees your eBay password.

Publishing to eBay says a policy or location is missing

eBay requires business policies for shipping, payment and returns. Create them in eBay Seller Hub → Business policies, then tap Refresh on the Account tab. If you have no item location, enter the ZIP code your items ship from and tap “Add location”.

How is the eBay category chosen?

Tap “Find eBay category” on the listing form to pick from eBay’s suggestions. If you don’t pick one, Resale Hub uses eBay’s top suggestion for your category and title when you publish. Some categories require extra item details; eBay’s message will tell you what’s missing.

Why can’t I publish to Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp or Depop?

Resale Hub only uses official marketplace APIs. Etsy is search-only because Etsy doesn’t grant listing access to apps like this one; the others don’t offer a public API. We never fake results.

How do I edit or delete a listing?

In the Listings tab, tap Edit or Delete on any listing. If the item is live on eBay, you can choose to end the eBay listing too. After editing a live item, publish it again to update eBay.

How do I delete my account?

Account → Delete account, then confirm with your password. See Delete your account for details.
