Delete your Resale Hub account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Resale Hub and go to the Account tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Permanently delete.

Without the app

Email mscofield1588@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Resale Hub account”. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Your sign-in account, all listings and their photo previews, and your eBay connection (tokens, policies and location settings). Server logs that may contain your IP address expire within 30 days. Listings that are already live on eBay remain on eBay, with their photos, and can be ended in eBay Seller Hub. Resale Hub does not keep its own copy of your photos: they are hosted by eBay Picture Services, which removes photos not used in a listing on its own.
